WHAT IT IS

One shared space for Central Yards and Adam Studios: the 2027 activation
calendar, projects, to-dos, ideas, quotes and receipts. Same design as the
Supply Co. site.


THE TABS

Overview — awaiting review, coming up, what is open.

Activation map — a month calendar like Google Calendar. Events sit on their day.
Arrows change month, Today jumps back, Month/Year/Quarter switch views. Click a
month name in Year view to open it. Drag an event to move it. Holidays, school
breaks and Lunar dates switch on and off.

Projects — two lanes: General (ongoing) and Special Projects. Each carries
Rapid / Standard / Big Ideas / Big Umbrella, plus links, owner and next action.

To-do — filtered by Everyone, ADAM or Central Yards. Tick to mark done. Done
items strike through but stay, so you can untick if you did it by mistake.
Ticks are the one thing the app itself writes.

Partnerships — partners and in-house ideas, linkable to a project or a moment.

Social — ADAM's brain dump. Marked ready when worth discussing with you.

Finance — Quotes, with reviewer and status. Receipts, where you drop a PDF or a
phone photo and add vendor, amount and project. Files are private; Open gives a
link that works for five minutes.

Decks — every deck and pitch, grouped by project, current one marked.


CURRENT DATA

Live, and real. Projects, activations and links come from the tracker Sheet;
tasks come from the ClickUp list "1600 - Central Yards Workspace". The sheet
refreshes when someone runs the importer, ClickUp every 15 minutes.

Two things to know:

- The app is the read surface. You cannot rename a project or add a task in the
  app. Change it in ClickUp or the Sheet, and it arrives on the next sync.
- Ticking a task in the app does stick. If ClickUp also changes that same task,
  the app's tick wins and the sync records the clash rather than overwriting it.


DAY TO DAY

What do I owe?  To-do, pick your team. Overdue is red.
What's coming?  Overview, or the Activation map.
File a receipt? Finance, Receipts, drop the file.
Move a date?   Activation map, drag the event.


IF SOMETHING IS WRONG

Code not working — make sure it is your team's code, they are different.
Workspace unavailable — a setup problem, send Shane a screenshot.
Upload refused — PDF, image or spreadsheet only, 10 MB max.
Expected item missing — it may be marked internal, which hides it from the
Central Yards view. The ADAM code sees everything.

Please keep the codes out of group chats and shared docs. If one needs to
change, Shane can replace it in minutes.
