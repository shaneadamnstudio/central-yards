CENTRAL YARDS × ADAM STUDIOS — SHARED WORKSPACE

What it is, how to get in, and how each part works.


────────────────────────────────
1. WHAT THIS IS
────────────────────────────────

A shared workspace for Central Yards and Adam Studios. It holds the 2027
activation calendar, the project pipeline, the to-do list, partnership ideas,
social thinking, quotes and receipts — all in one place, behind an access code.

It is built on the same design as the Supply Co. research site, so it should
feel familiar if you have used that one.

Live address: https://central-yards.vercel.app

It also has a calendar that works like Google Calendar, where you can see what
is happening on each day.

────────────────────────────────
3. THE EIGHT TABS
────────────────────────────────

OVERVIEW
Your starting point. It shows what is awaiting review, upcoming milestones,
the next activation, and how many tasks are open. Anything needing somebody's
attention shows here with who owes the next action, when it is due, and a
direct link to the document.

ACTIVATION MAP
A real month calendar. Each event sits on the day it happens, the way Google
Calendar works.

  - Use the arrows to move between months, or Today to jump back
  - Month / Year / By quarter switches the view
  - In Year view, click any month name to open that month in full
  - Click an event to see its details
  - Drag an event onto another day to move it. The duration is kept.
  - Public holidays, school holidays, Lunar and festival dates, and recurring
    dates can each be switched on and off

The coloured days are a key: each switch has a colour swatch matching the days
it highlights, so pink means a public holiday.

PROJECTS
The pipeline, in two lanes: General Central Yards Projects (ongoing work) and
Special Projects (standalone initiatives leading into 2027).

Each project keeps its own label — Rapid, Standard, Big Ideas or Big Umbrella —
and holds its links, owner, key dates and next action. Click a project to open
its panel and see every Google Doc and working file attached to it.

TO-DO
One list, filtered by Everyone, ADAM or Central Yards. Every task shows its
owner, due date and which project it belongs to.

Tick the box on the left to mark something done. A done task is struck through
and stays in place, so you can untick it if you did it by mistake. Use Show
done to see everything.

PARTNERSHIPS
Potential partners and in-house ADAM concepts for Central Yards — product
ideas, events, activations. Each idea can be linked to a project or a marketing
moment, so when a date firms up you can see which ideas were already built for
it.

SOCIAL
Where ADAM brain-dumps and develops content themes. Everything starts internal.
When an idea is ready to discuss, it is marked ready and appears on the Central
Yards side.

FINANCE
Two tabs.

  Quotes — each quote links to its project and carries who is reviewing it and
  a status: Submitted, Reviewed, Confirmed or On Hold.

  Receipts — file a receipt or invoice against a project. Drop the file in or
  choose it, add the vendor, amount, currency and date, and it is filed. You
  can use a PDF, a photo from your phone, or a spreadsheet, up to 10 MB.

  Files are private. Clicking Open gives you a link that works for five
  minutes, so a receipt cannot be forwarded by copying a link out of the page.

DECKS
Every deck and pitch link, grouped by project. The current one is marked, so
nobody presents last week's version. The same link also appears inside the
project panel.

The workspace is live and connected to its database, but the contents are

Real projects and tasks will come from the ClickUp and Google Sheet tracker

────────────────────────────────
5. USING IT DAY TO DAY
────────────────────────────────

  Checking what you owe — open To-do and pick your team. Overdue items show
  in red.

  Seeing what is coming — Overview for the next few things, or Activation map
  for the full month.

  Filing a receipt — Finance, Receipts tab, drop the file, fill in the amount
  and project.

  Adding a link to a project — open the project and use its documents section.

  Moving a marketing moment — Activation map, then drag the event onto its new
  day.


────────────────────────────────
6. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
────────────────────────────────

  The code will not work — check you are using the right team's code. ADAM and
  Central Yards codes are different and see different things.

  A page says the workspace is unavailable — that is a setup problem on the
  deployment, not something you can fix. Send a screenshot to Shane.

  An upload is refused — only PDF, image and spreadsheet files are accepted,
  and the limit is 10 MB.

  Something saved but looks wrong on another device — tell Shane which item
  and when. Saves are checked against the database, so a genuine mismatch is
  worth knowing about.

  You cannot see something you expect to — it may be marked internal, which
  hides it from the Central Yards view by design.
